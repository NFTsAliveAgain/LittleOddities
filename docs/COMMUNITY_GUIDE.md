# Community Guide

## Principles
Real people, real activity. We never invent members, votes, comments, winners or partnerships.
Everything public on the site comes from real records.

## Discord channel structure (proposed)
**WELCOME** — #rules · #announcements (team only) · #start-here · #official-links
**COMMUNITY** — #general · #introductions · #oddity-showcase · #fan-art · #memes
**THE ODDITIES** — #character-stories · #lore-ideas · #suggestions
**EVENTS** — #giveaways · #contests · #events
**HOLDERS** (role verified by a holder-verification bot, e.g. Collab.Land or Guild.xyz) — #holder-lounge · #holder-votes
**SUPPORT** — #help (staff will never DM first) · #report-scams

## Safety
Lock DMs from server members by default; pin the anti-scam message; moderators with 2FA;
slow-mode during launch; verification gate (captcha bot).

## Running polls & character votes
Create in admin → open → share link. Holder-only polls check the voter's NFT balance on-chain.
One vote per wallet. Results are public and live.

## Contests
Announce rules, dates, judging and prizes up front. Record winners in admin. Prizes from the 500 reserve.
